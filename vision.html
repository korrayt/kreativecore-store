How It Works

1

Analyze Footage

Select clip, sequence, or entire project. Vision analyzes with CLIP AI and InsightFace models - running locally on your GPU.

2

Search Naturally

Type what you're looking for: "happy crowd", "interior office", "sunset timelapse". Vision searches semantically, not just tags.

3

Review & Refine

Click any result to preview in Source Monitor. Double-click to load with In/Out points. Refine face/location tags as you work.

4

Generate Sequence

Hit "Create Sequence" (The Lifesaver). Vision generates a stringout with all search results, perfectly timed. Edit from there.
